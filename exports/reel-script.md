# squish reel — script and shot list

Version 2, reworked after watching real "I built a website" reels (listed at the bottom). What changed: the hook is now a time span, the screen is filmed with your phone instead of screen-recorded, captions are word-by-word in the middle, and it ends on a comment ask instead of "link in bio".

Target: 22–26 seconds, 9:16, voiceover + word-by-word captions. You film everything on your phone in one sitting, about 15 minutes.

## The story in one line

"I built my own mascot maker in a day, because the one I found only did one thing."

Both halves are true: the site was built in a single session on 12 November 2026, and it started from a site with one black blob.

## Hook — pick one (first 1.5 seconds)

1. "I built this in one day." *(recommended: a time span, like the reels that did best)*
2. "I built a mascot maker in a day, and I can't stop pressing this button."
3. "One day. One website. Infinite little guys."

Show the result while you say it: the mascot bouncing on your laptop, you in frame. Put the words on screen, big and centred.

## Script

| Time | Shot | What we see | You say | Caption on screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | **1. You + laptop** | You standing or sitting next to the laptop, mascot bouncing on screen. Look at the camera. | "I built this in one day." | I BUILT THIS IN ONE DAY |
| 0:02–0:05 | **2. Phone-filmed screen** | Phone pointed at the laptop: the original site with its one black blob. | "I found a mascot site I liked, but it only did this." | it only did this |
| 0:05–0:09 | **3. Phone-filmed screen** | Your finger or cursor hitting Shuffle five times. Let the camera shake a little. | "So I made my own. Every tap is a brand new character." | every tap = new character |
| 0:09–0:13 | **4. Phone-filmed screen** | Tap through shapes: star, cloud, heart. Move the phone closer. | "Fourteen shapes that melt into each other." | 14 shapes |
| 0:13–0:17 | **5. Phone-filmed screen** | Moods, then hats: laughing, angry, crown, bunny ears. | "Sixteen moods. And hats. Obviously hats." | 16 moods + hats |
| 0:17–0:20 | **6. Phone-filmed screen** | Tap Bounce, then open Export. | "It moves, and it exports as a video. Like this one." | exports as video |
| 0:20–0:24 | **7. You + laptop** | Back to you, mascot bouncing. Point at it or shrug. | "Comment 'mascot' and I'll send you the link." | comment MASCOT |

About 70 words, roughly 22 seconds at a normal pace. To get under 20 seconds, drop shot 6.

## How to film it

- **One location, one sitting.** Laptop on a desk by a window, brightness up, room lights off behind you so the screen doesn't glare.
- **Shots 1 and 7:** prop the phone on books at chest height, a step back, so you and the laptop are both in frame. Do the hook four or five times and keep the most relaxed one.
- **Shots 2–6:** hold the phone in one hand about 30 cm from the screen and tap with the other. Fill the frame with the mascot and the buttons you're pressing. Slight movement is good; it's what makes these reels feel real.
- **Voiceover:** record it in one take afterwards, in a quiet room, then lay the clips on top.
- **Captions:** use Instagram's or CapCut's auto-captions, set to word-by-word, bold, centred, and fix any wrong words.

If you don't want to show your face, use a phone-filmed shot of the bouncing mascot for shots 1 and 7 and keep the voiceover.

## Editing notes

- Cut on the action: every cut should land on a tap or a morph.
- Keep all text inside the middle of the frame; Instagram covers the top ~10% and bottom ~20%.
- Add a quiet instrumental under the voice inside Instagram, at about 15–20% volume.
- Cover frame: you next to the laptop with "I BUILT THIS IN ONE DAY" on it.

## Before you post

- The site has to be live before you can send anyone the link. It is not deployed yet.
- "Comment 'mascot'" means you reply to each comment with the link yourself, or set up an auto-reply tool. Have the link ready.
- If you'd rather not show your face, replace shot A with shot B and say the hook as voiceover over it.
